Playmaker

Five real pro moments a day. Pick the pass.

You have the ball for 3 seconds of a real match. Beat the AI coach's pick, then guess the pass the pro really played.

How to play
  1. Watch 3 seconds of real play. The ball carrier has a yellow ring; your team (red) attacks up the screen.
  2. What would you do? Tap a teammate to pass, a yellow chevron to carry, or the goal to shoot, then Confirm. Up to 100 points if the AI coach rates your choice among the best (a close call scores 100 too).
  3. What did the pro actually do? The pro passed in every moment: tap the teammate who got the ball. Right or wrong, checked against the real match: your Pro reads out of 5.
  4. Then watch what really happened. One game a day, same moments for everyone.
