2 · Calibrate the pitch

Pick 4 landmarks you can see (no 3 on one line), then tap them on the picture in order. "Near" is the touchline closest to the camera, "left" is the goal on the left of the TV picture.

    Passing options (experimental)

    Tap the ball carrier on the pitch above, then choose the attacking direction.

      Lane colour = distance from the nearest opponent to the straight pass line (open ≥ 4 m, tight ≥ 2 m, blocked < 2 m). A cheap proxy: it ignores speeds, pass height, body shape and players off the picture.

      Honest limits

      • Only what the camera sees: a TV picture is a partial view, players off screen are missing.
      • Small model (EfficientDet-Lite0, trained on everyday photos): small, overlapping or blurred players are missed, and people in the crowd or on the bench can be detected.
      • Feet = bottom of the box: occlusions and cut boxes move players on the map by a few metres.
      • Team colours come from the average shirt colour and can fail (similar kits, shadows, keepers, referees).
      • No ball detection yet, and the calibration is manual: one picture at a time, no video.
