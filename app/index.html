Five real moments from professional matches. Choose what the player should do, then see what the AI coach picked, what the pro did, and what really happened next.

How to play
  1. Watch 3 seconds of real play. The ball carrier has a yellow ring; your team (red) attacks up the screen.
  2. Tap a teammate to pass, a yellow arrow to carry the ball, or the goal to shoot. Then Confirm.
  3. 100 points if your choice is among the best according to the AI coach. When the coach cannot tell two options apart, both score 100.
  4. Then watch what really happened. One game a day, same moments for everyone.
